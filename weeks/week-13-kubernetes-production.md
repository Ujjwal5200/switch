**Priority:** P0 / AI specialization

## Learn

- 10 documents
- 1,000
- 10,000
- 100,000+ where practical

Study:
- chunking
- embedding batching
- metadata filtering
- ANN indexes
- hybrid retrieval
- reranking
- async ingestion
- tenant partitioning
- index versioning
- cache strategy
- evaluation datasets
